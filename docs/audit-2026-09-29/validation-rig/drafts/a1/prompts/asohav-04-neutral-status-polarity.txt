You are working in the ASoHaVCompanionApp repository (A Story of Heroes and Villains): a tabletop RPG companion app. A Hero's character sheet carries Statuses (Bleeding, Rattled, Prepared...), each with a Name, a Rank from 1 to 6 and a Polarity: Positive (helps the Hero), Negative (hurts the Hero) or Neutral (a note-like Status that is neither - the Give a Status dialog offers all three). packages/shared/src/engine.ts is the pure rules engine; the app never rolls dice, it tells the player what to roll. computeRollBreakdown(sheet, virtueId, library, moveId?) itemizes 2d6 + Virtue score plus the single highest helpful Status (added) and the single highest hindering Status (subtracted), returning Sources and Total. giveStatus(statuses, incoming, maxRank) adds or raises a Status, caps its Rank at maxRank (6) and returns { Statuses, Subdued }; Subdued: true means a Negative Status has just been pushed to the cap, so the player must run the Subdued flow (take a Scar, Risk Death or Blaze of Glory). There is a bug: both functions decide whether a Status is bad by asking whether it is not Positive instead of whether it is Negative, so a Neutral Status is treated as a Negative one in two independent places. A Rank 5 Neutral Status named Watched subtracts 5 from a roll total and is listed as the highest hindering Status, and a Neutral Status that reaches the cap wrongly reports Subdued: true. Fix engine.ts so that: (1) computeRollBreakdown never counts a Neutral Status as helpful or hindering - it adds no Status entry to Sources and changes nothing in Total, and next to a lower-ranked Negative Status it is still the Negative one that is reported as hindering; (2) giveStatus reports Subdued: true only when the incoming Status is Negative and reaches maxRank - a Neutral Status at the cap still has its Rank capped at maxRank but reports Subdued: false; (3) Positive and Negative Statuses behave exactly as before (the highest Positive is added, the highest Negative is subtracted, a Positive at the cap is not Subdued, a Negative at the cap is). The same Polarity test also appears in packages/shared/src/logic.ts; that one is out of scope here, leave it alone. Add or update tests in packages/shared/src/engine.test.ts (it already has makeSheet and makeStatus helpers and a seeded library) proving (1) and (2) for a Neutral Status. Your added/updated test(s) must FAIL if only your engine.ts fix is reverted while the test changes stay. Change ONLY these two files: packages/shared/src/engine.ts and packages/shared/src/engine.test.ts. Do not commit. Do not touch any other file.
