You are working in the ASoHaVCompanionApp repository (A Story of Heroes and Villains): a tabletop RPG companion app whose Content Admin authors a glossary of terms (Name, Aliases, Definition) that the character sheet turns into tap-to-reveal links. packages/shared/src/glossary.ts exports linkifyText(text, matcher, depth = 0, excludeTermId?), which splits text into segments of the form { text, term? }, where term is set only on a segment that links to a glossary term. Links come from two sources: automatic matches of a capitalized term Name or Alias, and explicit author tags written [Term] or [display][term-id-or-name], e.g. 'you feel [shaken][g-condition] by it.' shows 'shaken' as a link to the term g-condition. depth counts bubble-opens: 0 is the original sheet text, and a term's Definition, shown inside the bubble opened by tapping a depth-0 link, is linkified at depth 1. The constant MAX_DEPTH is meant to stop a bubble from opening further bubbles, but there are two defects. (a) It is 1, so a Definition inside a bubble can itself contain links and a player can tap Kin, then Bond, then Kin again, stacking bubbles without end; the intent was always exactly one level, so text at depth 1 must be plain. (b) Past the limit the function returns the raw text without scanning for tags at all, so an explicit tag inside such a Definition shows its literal brackets. Fix linkifyText so that: (1) at depth 0 everything behaves exactly as today, including auto-linking, explicit tags and excludeTermId; (2) at depth 1 and deeper no segment has a term, and text with no brackets comes back with its content unchanged; (3) at depth 1 and deeper an explicit tag is flattened to its plain display text instead of leaking brackets, so 'you feel [shaken][g-condition] by it.' yields segments whose text joins to 'you feel shaken by it.', while escaped brackets, an unclosed bracket and an unresolved tag keep rendering as they do at depth 0. The existing test 'does not link past MAX_DEPTH' encodes the old limit (depth 1 links, depth 2 does not), so update it to the new one. Add or update tests in packages/shared/src/glossary.test.ts proving: depth 0 links and depth 1 does not; and an explicit [display][id] tag at depth 1 is flattened to its display text with no linked segment.
