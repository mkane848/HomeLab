#### **<5> | PASS | Evidence supports the color identity behavior**
- **Evidence:** The plan states that color identity is checked over the whole deck using the decoded `color_identity` fields — matching CR 903.4.

#### **<6> | PASS | Evidence supports the commander unit legality**

#### **<7> | PASS | Evidence supports the legality surface reporting**
- **Evidence:** The plan says that banned and unparseable cards are reported and fail the verdict — not silently dropped.
- **Supporting facts:** Unit tests for banned and unparseable cards (e.g., "a banned card still occupies a deck slot") verify this.
